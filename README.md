<h2>✦ About Me</h2>

<p>
  I'm a software developer with a background in design and a slightly unhealthy obsession with making things look nice.
</p>

<p>
  I have a <strong>BA in Creative Technologies</strong>, specialising in Interactive Development, and I love the space where creativity and logic meet. I enjoy solving the technical problems behind a project just as much as I enjoy figuring out how it should look, feel and move.
</p>

<p>
  I'm especially drawn to <strong>frontend development</strong>, but I enjoy full-stack work too. I currently work mostly with <strong>React, TypeScript and NestJS</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  My design background has definitely followed me into development — I care about the little details, from layouts and typography to interactions and the overall feel of an experience.
</p>

<p>
  Basically, I like making things <strong>work beautifully</strong>. ♡
</p>

<br>

<h2>🎨 The Creative Side</h2>

<p>
  I love the overlap between design and development. I'm happiest when I get to use both sides of my brain — the logical problem-solving side and the creative side.
</p>

<br>

<p>✦ Creating clean, thoughtful interfaces</p>
<p>✦ Turning ideas into interactive experiences</p>
<p>✦ Branding and visual identity</p>
<p>✦ Typography, colour and all the tiny details</p>
<p>✦ Making websites feel a little more <i>✨special✨</i></p>
<p>✦ Taking something functional and making it pretty</p>
